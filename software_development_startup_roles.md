# Mga Role sa Software Development Startup Company

Sa isang **Software Development Startup Company**, may iba't ibang roles depende sa laki ng company, dami ng employees, at uri ng software na ginagawa. Kung startup na gumagawa ng web apps, mobile apps, desktop applications, AI integration, at custom software, ito ang mga karaniwang roles.

## 1. Management at Leadership Roles

### 1. CEO (Chief Executive Officer)
Pinuno ng company. Siya ang nagdedesisyon sa business direction, company goals, partnerships, at overall strategy.

- **Focus:** Business leadership at company growth

### 2. CTO (Chief Technology Officer)
Namamahala sa technology direction, software architecture, technical standards, at technology stack ng company.

- **Focus:** Technical leadership at innovation

### 3. COO (Chief Operating Officer)
Nangangasiwa sa daily operations, workflows, team coordination, at delivery ng projects.

- **Focus:** Operations at productivity

**Paalala:** Hindi kailangang may CEO, CTO, at COO agad ang startup. Puwedeng iisang founder muna ang humawak ng dalawa o higit pang responsibilities.

## 2. Software Development Team

Ito ang pangunahing team na gumagawa ng software products at client projects.

### 4. Software Development Lead / Tech Lead
Gumagabay sa developers, nagre-review ng code, at nagsisiguro na maayos ang technical implementation.

### 5. Full-Stack Developer
Gumagawa ng frontend at backend, kabilang ang user interface, APIs, database integration, at business logic.

### 6. Frontend Developer
Responsable sa nakikita at ginagamit ng users, tulad ng pages, forms, navigation, responsiveness, at interactive components.

### 7. Backend Developer
Gumagawa ng server-side logic, APIs, authentication, database operations, at integrations.

### 8. Mobile App Developer
Gumagawa ng Android at iOS applications gamit ang Flutter, React Native, Kotlin, Swift, o iba pang technologies.

### 9. UI/UX Designer
Nagdidisenyo ng wireframes, prototypes, layouts, design systems, at user experience bago o habang ginagawa ang application.

## 3. Quality Assurance at Technical Infrastructure

| Role | Pangunahing responsibilidad |
|---|---|
| **QA Engineer / Software Tester** | Sinusubukan ang software para matukoy ang bugs at i-verify na gumagana ang requirements. |
| **DevOps Engineer** | Deployment, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, monitoring, at automation. |
| **Database Administrator (DBA)** | Database performance, backups, maintenance, at access management. |
| **Cybersecurity Engineer** | Security testing, vulnerability prevention, at protection ng systems at data. |
| **AI/ML Engineer** | AI integrations, machine learning models, data pipelines, at intelligent features. |

Sa maliit na startup, maaaring ang developers mismo ang gumagawa ng testing, deployment, database maintenance, at basic security.

## 4. Project Management at Client Relations

| Role | Pangunahing responsibilidad |
|---|---|
| **Project Manager (PM)** | Timeline, budget, tasks, deadlines, at coordination ng team. |
| **Business Analyst (BA)** | Kinukuha at sinusuri ang client requirements at ginagawang malinaw na system specifications. |
| **Product Manager** | Nagpapasya kung anong features ang uunahin at kung paano matutugunan ang needs ng users. |
| **Business Development Manager** | Naghahanap ng clients, partnerships, at business opportunities. |
| **Sales / Account Manager** | Client inquiries, proposals, quotations, negotiations, at client relationships. |
| **Customer Support / Technical Support** | Tumutulong sa users at nag-aasikaso ng reported issues. |

**Pagkakaiba ng Project Manager at Product Manager:** Ang Project Manager ay nakatuon sa **paano at kailan matatapos ang project**, samantalang ang Product Manager ay nakatuon sa **ano ang dapat buuin at bakit ito mahalaga**.

## 5. Administrative at Business Support

| Role | Pangunahing responsibilidad |
|---|---|
| **HR / Recruitment** | Hiring, onboarding, employee records, at team development. |
| **Finance / Accountant** | Budget, payroll, invoices, expenses, at financial reports. |
| **Legal / Compliance** | Contracts, intellectual property, privacy, at legal requirements. |
| **Marketing Specialist** | Branding, social media, website marketing, at lead generation. |

Hindi lahat ng startup ay may sariling empleyado para sa mga ito. Maaari munang outsourced o part-time ang accounting, legal, at recruitment.

## 6. Recommended Organizational Structure para sa Software Startup

Kung magsisimula ng maliit na software development company, hindi kailangang i-hire agad ang lahat ng roles.

```text
Founder / CEO
│
├── Tech Lead / CTO
│   ├── Full-Stack / Frontend / Backend Developers
│   ├── Mobile App Developer
│   ├── DevOps / Database / Security (as needed)
│   └── QA Engineer / Software Tester
│
├── Project / Business Lead
│   ├── Project Manager
│   ├── Business Analyst
│   ├── Product Manager (if needed)
│   └── Sales / Business Development
│
└── UI/UX Designer
    └── Marketing / Customer Support / Admin (as needed)
```

*Illustrative structure lamang. Puwedeng pagsamahin ang ilang roles depende sa team size.*

## 7. Halimbawa ng Lean Startup Team

Para sa startup na nagsisimula pa lang at tumatanggap ng custom software projects, puwedeng ganito ang initial team:

| Bilang | Position | Pangunahing trabaho |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Founder / CEO | Business, sales, at client relationships |
| 1 | Tech Lead / Full-Stack Developer | Architecture at core development |
| 1 | Full-Stack Developer | Frontend, backend, at integrations |
| 1 | UI/UX Designer + QA | Design at software testing |
| 1 | Project Manager / Business Analyst | Requirements, scope, at deadlines |

**Total: 5 people.** Maaari pang mas maliit dito kung founder-led at freelance-based muna ang operations.

Ang mahalaga ay may malinaw na owner ang bawat responsibility, hindi na may hiwalay na empleyado para sa bawat role.

## 8. Mga Dapat Isaalang-alang sa Pagbuo ng Team

- **Unahin ang essential roles.** Sa simula, maaaring sapat na ang founder, developer(s), at isang taong humahawak ng design, testing, o project coordination.
- **Pagsamahin ang compatible roles.** Halimbawa, puwedeng maging Tech Lead at Full-Stack Developer ang iisang tao.
- **Mag-outsource kung kailangan.** Accounting, legal services, at recruitment ay puwedeng kunin mula sa external providers.
- **Ihiwalay ang responsibilities kahit pinagsama ang roles.** Dapat malinaw kung sino ang may pananagutan sa client communication, code quality, testing, deployment, at deadlines.
- **Magdagdag ng specialists habang lumalaki.** Kapag dumami ang projects at users, maaaring kailanganin ang dedicated QA, DevOps, security, product, at customer support personnel.

## 9. Checklist para sa Pagpaplano ng Startup

Gamitin ang mga tanong na ito para matukoy ang angkop na organizational structure:

1. **Business model:** Custom software agency, SaaS product startup, o pareho?
2. **Team size:** 1–2, 3–5, 6–10, o higit sa 10 tao?
3. **Founder role:** Technical, business-focused, o pareho?
4. **Software focus:** Web applications, mobile applications, desktop applications, AI integration, game development, Web3/blockchain, o kombinasyon?
5. **Hiring strategy:** Full-time employees, freelancers, outsourced specialists, o hybrid?

## Konklusyon

Hindi kailangang magkaroon agad ng malaking organizational structure ang isang software development startup. Mas mahalaga ang malinaw na responsibilities, maayos na project workflow, kalidad ng software, at kakayahang makakuha at makapaglingkod sa clients. Habang lumalaki ang company, saka magdagdag ng dedicated roles batay sa pangangailangan ng negosyo.
